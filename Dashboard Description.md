# 📊 Dashboard Description

## New York City Airbnb Market Performance Analysis

The **New York City Airbnb Market Performance Analysis** dashboard is an interactive Tableau visualization designed to explore pricing trends, neighborhood performance, and customer engagement across Airbnb listings in New York City. The dashboard combines geographic mapping with comparative charts to help users understand how listing prices vary across boroughs and how pricing relates to customer reviews.

### 🗺️ NYC Airbnb Map

The dashboard features an interactive map that displays Airbnb listings across New York City's five boroughs:

* Bronx
* Brooklyn
* Manhattan
* Queens
* Staten Island

Listings are color-coded by **Neighbourhood Group**, allowing users to quickly identify their geographic distribution and explore regional market patterns.

### 📊 Average Price by Borough

A bar chart compares the **average listing price** across different boroughs. This visualization helps identify which areas command higher accommodation prices and highlights pricing differences between neighborhoods.

### 📈 Price vs Reviews Analysis

The line chart visualizes the relationship between **Price** and **Reviews Per Month**, enabling users to examine whether higher-priced listings receive more customer engagement or whether affordability influences review activity.

### 🎨 Interactive Features

The dashboard includes:

* Borough-wise color coding for easy comparison.
* Interactive geographic visualization using Tableau Maps.
* Comparative bar chart for average pricing analysis.
* Price and review relationship analysis through a line chart.
* Dynamic legends for neighborhood filtering and interpretation.

## Business Purpose

The dashboard helps property owners, travelers, and market analysts understand:

* Which boroughs have the highest average Airbnb prices.
* Geographic distribution of Airbnb listings across New York City.
* The relationship between listing price and customer reviews.
* Regional pricing patterns that can support pricing strategies and investment decisions.

By combining spatial analysis with pricing and customer engagement metrics, the dashboard provides a concise overview of the New York City Airbnb marketplace and supports data-driven decision-making.
